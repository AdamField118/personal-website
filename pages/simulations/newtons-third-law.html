The Physics

Forces always come in pairs: if object A pushes on object B with force F, then B pushes back on A with force −F. The two forces act on different objects, which is why they don’t cancel.

A direct consequence is conservation of momentum: the momentum gained by one object is exactly the momentum lost by the other.

Things to Try

  • Compare the recoil of a light object pushing off a heavy one — equal forces, very different accelerations.
  • Add up the two momenta before and after the interaction and check the total is unchanged.
