The Physics

Capacitors combine the opposite way to resistors: in parallel the plate areas effectively add, so Ceq = C1 + C2 + …; in series the same charge sits on every capacitor and 1/Ceq = 1/C1 + 1/C2 + ….

The reason is what adds in each case: in parallel the capacitors share the voltage and the charges add; in series they share the charge and the voltages add (Q = CV).

Things to Try

  • Check that two equal capacitors in series give half the capacitance of one.
  • Follow where the charge accumulates in each configuration.
