The Physics

At an interface between two media, part of a light ray reflects (angle of reflection equals angle of incidence) and part refracts according to Snell’s law: n1 sin θ1 = n2 sin θ2.

When light travels from a denser to a less dense medium, there is a critical angle θc = arcsin(n2/n1) beyond which no refracted ray exists and the light is totally internally reflected — the principle behind optical fibres.

Things to Try

  • Increase the incident angle slowly and find the critical angle where the refracted ray vanishes.
  • Compare how much the ray bends entering a denser medium versus leaving it.
