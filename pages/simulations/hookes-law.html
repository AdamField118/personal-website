The Physics

A spring pulls back with force proportional to its stretch: F = −kx. That linear restoring force produces simple harmonic motion with angular frequency ω = √(k/m) and period T = 2π√(m/k).

Almost any system near a stable equilibrium behaves like this for small displacements, which is why the harmonic oscillator shows up everywhere from molecular vibrations to circuit oscillations.

Things to Try

  • Quadruple the mass and confirm the period doubles.
  • Note that the period does not depend on the amplitude you start with.
