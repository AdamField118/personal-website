The Physics

A changing magnetic flux through a circuit induces an electromotive force: ℰ = −dΦB/dt. Only the change matters — a stationary magnet induces nothing.

The minus sign is Lenz’s law: the induced current flows in the direction that opposes the change creating it, a direct consequence of energy conservation. This is the operating principle of generators, transformers, and induction stoves.

Things to Try

  • Move the magnet quickly versus slowly and compare the induced current.
  • Flip the magnet’s direction of motion and watch the current reverse, as Lenz’s law demands.
