The Physics

The line from the Sun to a planet sweeps out equal areas in equal times. A planet therefore moves fastest at perihelion (closest approach) and slowest at aphelion.

The law is nothing but conservation of angular momentum: gravity points along the Sun–planet line, exerts no torque, and so L = mr2 dθ/dt stays constant — the swept-area rate is L/2m.

Things to Try

  • Compare the planet’s speed at the two ends of an eccentric orbit.
  • Check that the shaded area segments are equal even though their shapes differ.
