The Physics

Without friction, mechanical energy is constant: E = ½mv2 + mgh. A falling object trades height for speed at exactly the rate needed to keep E fixed.

Air resistance and imperfect bounces steadily drain mechanical energy into heat, which is why each bounce is lower than the last.

Things to Try

  • Turn air resistance off and check the ball returns to its original height each bounce.
  • Watch the kinetic and potential energy bars: their sum stays constant in the ideal case.
