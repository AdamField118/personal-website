The Physics

Two point charges exert forces along the line joining them with magnitude F = kq1q2/r2, repulsive for like charges, attractive for opposite ones. It has the same inverse-square form as gravity but is enormously stronger and comes in two signs.

Forces from multiple charges simply add as vectors (superposition), which is what makes the electric field such a useful concept.

Things to Try

  • Halve the distance between two charges and confirm the force quadruples.
  • Set up three charges and watch how the net force on one is the vector sum of two pair forces.
