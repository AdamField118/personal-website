The Physics

The net force on an object determines its acceleration: F = ma. Doubling the force doubles the acceleration; doubling the mass halves it.

Note that force controls the change in velocity, not the velocity itself — a constant force produces steadily increasing speed, not constant speed.

Things to Try

  • Apply the same force to different masses and compare the accelerations.
  • Watch the velocity keep growing while the force (and thus acceleration) stays constant.
