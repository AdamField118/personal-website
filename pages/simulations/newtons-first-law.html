The Physics

An object keeps a constant velocity unless a net external force acts on it. Everyday objects appear to “naturally” slow down only because friction and drag are forces too.

In this simulation you can remove or vary those forces and see that with zero net force the motion really is unchanging — Galileo’s insight that Newton made the foundation of mechanics.

Things to Try

  • Set friction to zero, give the object a push, and watch it coast forever.
  • Balance two applied forces and confirm the object behaves exactly as if there were no force at all.
