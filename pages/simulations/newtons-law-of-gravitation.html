The Physics

Every pair of masses attracts with force F = Gm1m2/r2. The same law governs a falling apple and the Moon’s orbit — Newton’s great unification.

An orbit is simply continuous free fall: the orbiting body accelerates toward the central mass but keeps missing it because of its sideways velocity. Too slow and it spirals in; too fast and it escapes.

Things to Try

  • Vary the initial sideways velocity and find the value giving a circular orbit.
  • Watch how the speed increases near closest approach on an elliptical orbit.
