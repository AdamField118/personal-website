The Physics

For small angles a pendulum is a simple harmonic oscillator with period T = 2π√(L/g) — independent of the mass and, remarkably, of the amplitude. That independence is what made pendulum clocks possible.

At larger angles the restoring force is no longer proportional to the displacement and the period grows with amplitude; damping slowly converts the energy sloshing between kinetic and potential into heat.

Things to Try

  • Double the length and check the period grows by a factor of √2.
  • Compare the period at 5° and 60° amplitude to see the small-angle approximation break down.
