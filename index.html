Projects & Research

Below you can find a bunch of projects I've done that I am proud of!

ShearNet

A neural network-based galaxy shear estimation with JAX GPU acceleration, simulation, and benchmarking tools. Implementing deconvolution neural network for metacalibration, drastically improving bias estimation.

PH3999 - Computational Physics Independent Study

In this course we will study and implement the mathematics and code behind Finite Element Method and Finite Volume Method. See the website I have to host my work!

Modeling of a Chaotic Pendulum

In Intermediate Physics Laboratory (2025) we have a project to analyze a chaotic pendulum system. This will be done through tracking the angles of a physical set up, making a theoretical model, then comparing the results of the two.

Computational Thermodynamics

Extending research on community formation, implementing zero-sum game simulations. Working on alternative characterizations of system entropy, new network topologies, and better visualizations of evolution.

Physics Simulations!

A dream since I was in High School, I always wanted to make my own physics simulation website inspired by popular ones used in classes. This is where that dream is slowly becoming a reality!

An Overview of Weak Gravitational Lensing

For a final project I interviewed an expert in my research field of interest and included real results from weak lensing pipelines.

Dynamic Resume!

I made this while learning three.js and webGL shaders!

How to use SRS Synthesized Function Generator and RIGOL Oscilloscope

For my Oscillation & Waves course as a project alternative to quiz 3

Alienation from Natures: Producing the Ecological Subject

For a capstone project in humanities, I wrote a paper that argues ecological destruction under capitalism is not only material but subjective: capitalism produces ecological subjects whose perceptual habits and practices normalize instrumental relations to the natural world.
